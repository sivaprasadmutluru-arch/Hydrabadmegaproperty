When and where is the expo?

Planned for 2027 in Hyderabad. Exact dates and the proposed HITEX venue are awaiting confirmation. Confirm the final details before booking travel.

How do I get my pass?

Register as a visitor, delegate or exhibitor. Save your application ID and print your digital QR pass. You can retrieve the pass with Find My Pass.

How are exhibitor payments handled?

The organizer confirms your quotation and stall availability. A configured payment link opens your provider's hosted checkout. Payments are recorded after organizer verification; automatic payment processing is not connected.

Are speakers and sponsors confirmed?

Confirmed participants will be announced here. Current partnership placements are opportunities to enquire, rather than confirmed sponsors.
